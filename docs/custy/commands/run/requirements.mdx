## Overview

Requirements depend on the selected profile. A combined sequence inherits the
requirements and side effects of every included stage.

<InfoBox
    variant="warning"
    title="Profiles combine requirements"
>

When multiple profiles are supplied, Custy merges their steps. The resulting
pipeline requires the union of their files, validation conditions, and external
access.

</InfoBox>
